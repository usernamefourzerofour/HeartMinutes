# Heart Minutes

Heart Minutes turns the heart rate from your Apple Watch into one simple number: minutes in your heart rate zones,
weighted like the American Heart Association recommends (moderate × 1, vigorous × 2), measured against a weekly goal of 150.
Workouts count, and so does everything in between: your Apple Watch measures your heart rate in the background all day,
and Heart Minutes logs it automatically – a brisk walk or cycling to work counts without you starting a workout.

## Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue: <https://github.com/usernamefourzerofour/HeartMinutes/issues>

**The number seems low or high?** Heart Minutes only counts minutes in your heart rate zones. Check your age or
maximum heart rate under Settings, and which measurements count (background, workouts) at the top of Settings.

**No data?** Make sure Heart Minutes has access in the Health app → Profile → Apps → Heart Minutes.

Heart Minutes is not a medical device.
