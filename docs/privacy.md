# Heart Minutes – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

Heart Minutes does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Health data stays on your device.** The app reads heart rate, workouts and exercise minutes from Apple Health
  to calculate your Heart Minutes. The data is processed only on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
- **Nothing is sent anywhere.** The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- **Settings** (goal, age, heart rate zones) are stored locally on your devices and synced between your iPhone and
  Apple Watch directly.
- **Health data is never written** to Apple Health, and it is never used for advertising or sold.

You can remove the app's access to Apple Health at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Heart Minutes is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any disease.

Heart Minutes is open source: <https://github.com/usernamefourzerofour/HeartMinutes>
